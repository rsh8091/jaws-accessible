RELEASE 1, CANDIDATE 1 (1.0-RC1)
September 30, 2026

SCOPE
Accessible single-game play: team and rule setup, numbered coaching menus,
passing and shooting decisions, substitutions, timeouts, halftime, overtime,
and accessible box scores. Season and tournament accessibility are outside
this release candidate's scope.

RECENT IMPROVEMENTS INCLUDED
- Halftime options 6 and 7 change offensive and defensive strategy.
- Changes return to halftime options and preserve human coaching choices.
- Both teams' actual second-half lineups are announced after computer coaching.
- Press Enter after the lineups to begin second-half play.
- Numbered Back and Cancel navigation for substitutions.
- Substitution bench-rest recovery and timeout-resume improvements.
- Updated Accessibility Help.
- Installation guidance for Windows warnings when running this unsigned build.
- Retains the previous beta installation process: download Jason's team data
  with "1 Install Team Data.cmd", then launch our accessible game.

VALIDATION
The included executable is the September 30 build that passed six halftime
scenarios, the substitution-navigation suite, strategy-ownership checks, and
the human-versus-computer transcript test. Final hands-on JAWS testing is still
needed. Packaging checks cover the manifest, checksum, launcher and offline
team-data installation. They do not validate a fresh internet download.

The free-throw shooter reproduction also confirmed an unresolved defect;
it is documented in KNOWN ISSUES.txt, not fixed in this candidate.

This is a local candidate package. No GitHub release or release page has been
published as part of preparing it.
