RELEASE 1, CANDIDATE 5 (1.0-RC5)
October 23, 2026

SCOPE
Accessible single-game play: team and rule setup, numbered coaching menus,
passing and shooting decisions, substitutions, timeouts, halftime, overtime,
and accessible box scores. Season and tournament accessibility are outside
this release candidate's scope.

NEW SINCE RC4
- Returning from gameplay now says "Returning to the main menu."
- Rebased onto upstream main at a0e9407 (October 10). The upstream update
  adjusts schedule date/time defaults and comments. Previous accessibility
  and integration fixes are preserved. Schedule-editor changes have not
  been manually tested; accessible gameplay is the focus of this release.
- Rebuilt the game and reran automated gameplay and installer checks.

PREVIOUS IMPROVEMENTS INCLUDED
- Numbered setup, lineup, coaching and substitution menus, with Back and Cancel.
- Screen-reader-friendly play-by-play, clock/status reports and box scores.
- Coaching options before every overtime, plus an overtime practice launcher.
- Offense and defense confirmations pause for Enter during play.
- Shorter Accessibility Help focused on game controls.
- Halftime strategy changes and final lineup announcements.
- Substitution bench-rest recovery and timeout-resume improvements.
- Missing-data startup guidance that waits for acknowledgment.
- Team-data installation with keep, reinstall and cancel choices, checksum
  verification, and supported-filename validation.

VALIDATION AND LIMITATIONS
The October 23 build passed all 18 gameplay test scripts, covering menus,
setup, team selection, narration, clock controls, strategy ownership, halftime,
overtime, substitutions and recovery, timeouts, intentional fouls, late-game
choices, box scores and older-team pacing.
The source-level intentional-foul regression and both installer test scripts
also passed. Installer checks covered fresh install, keep, cancel, invalid
input, reinstall, partial-install repair, rejected archives, and the official
team archive with its pinned checksum. Package checks verify contents,
launchers, checksum and offline team-data installation.

The tester completed a full game with RC5 and reported that it went fine.
This records the full-game result; it does not imply that every checklist
item or known simulation defect was exercised. See TESTING AND
FEEDBACK.txt for the checklist and KNOWN ISSUES.txt for existing simulation
defects, including free-throw shooter substitution. These reported defects
have not been fixed by this packaging update.

This is a local candidate package. No GitHub release has been published as
part of preparing it.
