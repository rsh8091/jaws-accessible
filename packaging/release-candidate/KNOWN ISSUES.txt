KNOWN ISSUES - RELEASE 1, CANDIDATE 1

FREE-THROW SHOOTER SUBSTITUTION
Do not substitute for the player with free throws pending. The replacement
can take the shots using their own free-throw rating and receive the statistics.
Confirmed in controlled executable tests of two shots and one-and-one.
https://github.com/jleonard2099/LHG_CollegeBB/issues/43

OTHER REPORTED SIMULATION ISSUES
- Steal checks can use another roster player's rating after lineup changes.
- An emergency computer-substitution fallback can select the same player for
  multiple positions when fewer than five players pass foul screening.
These were confirmed by source tracing, not full-game reproductions. Their
frequency and contribution to high turnover totals have not been established.
https://github.com/jleonard2099/LHG_CollegeBB/issues/42
- A computer defender can remain on court after fouling out in a bonus-state
  path. Upstream report: https://github.com/jleonard2099/LHG_CollegeBB/issues/40
- Computer substitutions may omit bench-rest recovery applied to human subs.
  Source-inspection report: https://github.com/jleonard2099/LHG_CollegeBB/issues/41

FINAL ACCESSIBILITY REVIEW
The new halftime controls and second-half lineup announcements passed automated

under stricter policies. See WINDOWS SECURITY PROMPTS in README FIRST.txt.

block it. See README FIRST.txt for platform support and installation details.
