BETA TESTING AND FEEDBACK
=========================

Please try complete games, including team selection, substitutions, timeouts,
halftime, box scores, and overtime when possible. The accessible mode is the main
focus of this beta.

Report problems by creating a new issue at:
https://github.com/rsh8091/jaws-accessible/issues

Please include:
- Windows version and whether it is 64-bit.
- Screen reader name and exact version. At the very least, if you use JAWS 2025, for example, we'll need to know that
- Visiting and home teams, including season years.
- The choices or keys used immediately before the problem.
- What you heard, what you expected, and whether play could continue.
- Whether the problem happens every time or only sometimes.

For narration problems, quote only the shortest useful part of the repeated,
missing, or unexpected announcement.

Do not include private information in a public GitHub issue.
