REPORTING ISSUES - VERSION 1.0

We welcome reports of gameplay, installation and accessibility problems:
https://github.com/rsh8091/jaws-accessible/issues

Please include what you can:
- Game version (1.0).
- Windows and screen-reader versions.
- Teams and season years, rules, and which team you controlled.
- The menu choices or actions leading to the problem.
- What happened, what you expected, and whether play could continue.
- Whether it repeats; a short transcript or box score can help.

Read KNOWN ISSUES.txt for problems already documented.
Do not include private information in a public issue.
